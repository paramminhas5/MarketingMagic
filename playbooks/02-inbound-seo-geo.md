## Dead or overrated
- **Informational blog farms.** Pew (68,879 searches): 8% click rate with an AI summary vs 15% without. AI Mode zero-click 93% (Semrush via Omnibound).
- **Pure-AI content at scale.** Not penalised as such, but underperforms on impressions and citations (Graphite; Ahrefs 2026).
- **Open rate as KPI.** Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates it (beehiiv; Ethan Brooks).
- **Paid Boosts as "growth."** Boosts average 84 subscribers a month (beehiiv 2026); paid referral lists can look explosive without engagement (Ethan Brooks).

## Stack
Ahrefs/Semrush (AIO filter), Profound/Peec AI, Google Search Console, GA4 AI-referral segment, beehiiv, HDYHAU field.

## Open questions and contradictions
- **Size of the AIO hit.** Ahrefs says the gap is widening (–58%); Seer saw AIO CTR rebound from 1.31% to 2.36% (Dec 2025 → Feb 2026). Verdict: Ahrefs is web-wide desktop data; Seer is client data with no causal claim. Plan on 35–60% loss for informational top-3 positions; treat the rebound as upside.
- **AI content and rankings.** Ahrefs "no correlation" and Graphite "human dominates" are compatible: AI-*assisted* is fine, AI-*only* struggles.
- **Engines source differently** (ChatGPT Wikipedia-heavy, Perplexity Reddit-heavy), and Google Search/AIO/Gemini source overlap is low (Grossman et al. 2026, cited in arXiv 2609.23162). Track each engine separately.
