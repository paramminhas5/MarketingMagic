## Dead or overrated
- **Narrow interest stacking and lookalike micro-segmentation on Meta.** Meta's own guidance has moved away.
- **"Diversity" via colour or CTA tweaks.** Meta treats near-duplicates as one creative.
- **Judging TLAs by total CTR.** 91% of TLA clicks never reach a landing page (4.56% total vs 0.43% landing-page CTR; Kiin). Landing-page CTR equals company-page ads (0.449% vs 0.441%); the premium is CPM ($74.71 vs $48.80).
- **Platform-reported ROAS as truth** (Wicked Reports).

## Stack
Meta Ads Manager (Advantage+, Creative diversity column), Confect/Motion, WhatsApp Business API (AiSensy, Interakt, Gupshup) with Flows, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, Google Ads.

## Open questions and contradictions
- **TLA performance.** Fractional Demand claims 4.65% vs 0.68% CTR (6.9x); Kiin finds identical landing-page CTR. Verdict: trust Kiin, a larger within-account dataset on the metric that matters.
- **Advantage+.** Meta reports +22% returns; Wicked Reports shows new-customer cost doubling. Verdict: run it, but exclude or cap existing customers and validate with a holdout.
- **TikTok ads.** No 2025–26 primary TikTok ads CAC or creative benchmark was gathered; this is a gap.
