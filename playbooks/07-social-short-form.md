## Dead or overrated
- **Single-image Instagram posts** (–18% YoY).
- **LinkedIn hashtags:** 3–5 slightly hurt, 6+ seriously hurt (AuthoredUp, van der Blom).
- **LinkedIn polls as reach hack:** multiplier 1.64x → 1.19x, median reach –35% (AuthoredUp).
- **AI comment pods:** van der Blom says about 80% of early comments on his posts are AI-written; dwell and saves now matter.

## Stack
Socialinsider/Rival IQ, AuthoredUp/Shield, CapCut/Descript, Metricool/Buffer.

## Open questions and contradictions
- **Carousels vs Reels.** Fanpage Karma: carousels get 51% more interactions; Socialinsider shows a small edge. Verdict: carousels engage, Reels reach; run both.
- **TikTok's 3.70% → 2.60% drop** compares different samples (198M posts vs 69M videos); the size is uncertain.
- **YouTube Shorts and X** have thin data (Shorts 0.30%, X 0.12%). Treat Shorts as cheap redistribution and X as low priority outside tech/creator niches.
- **LinkedIn video:** van der Blom/Right Angle say declining; AuthoredUp shows slight above-baseline reach. Use video for trust, not reach.
