## Dead or overrated
- **"Sales creates the preference."** Sales mostly confirms it; the pre-contact favourite wins about 80% (6sense, 2023–2026). Independent research share fell from 70% to 60% in 2025, but the decision still forms first.
- **Judging demand creation by last-touch attribution** (Refine Labs: 79% vs 3% for web search).
- **Brand-page-only LinkedIn.** Van der Blom 2025: reach down about 50%, follower growth down 59%.

## Stack
LinkedIn (personal profiles + TLAs), podcast hosting, Reddit/community, beehiiv, creator marketplaces, 6sense/Common Room, HubSpot "how did you hear about us" (HDYHAU) field.

## Open questions and contradictions
- **LLM use vs discovery:** 94% of buyers use LLMs, but only 26% use them to *discover* vendors (6sense). Verdict: AI shapes evaluation more than shortlisting; earn the shortlist through brand and people first.
- **Category creation:** no 2025–26 primary data surfaced. Treat it as an unproven bet.
- **Creator ROI:** anecdotal and vendor-skewed; no controlled benchmark found.
