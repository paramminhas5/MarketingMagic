## What still needs a human
- **Disclosed or synthetic-looking output:** in the NYU/Emory field study, disclosing AI involvement cut CTR by about 31.5% versus unlabelled human ads. AI-looking ads also underperform (Taboola).
- **Cold email voice:** Hunter 2026 found 69% of decision-makers are bothered by AI-written email "unless the output feels genuinely human," reversing its 2025 finding.
- **Differentiation and strategy:** 52–53% say AI makes content less effective or harder to differentiate (HubSpot 2026, via SlideShare mirror).
- **Hybrid edits:** AI-edited human ads sometimes did *worse* (NYU/Emory); human-AI teams produced better text but worse images (arXiv 2503.18238).

## Dead or overrated
- **"Fully autonomous marketing department."** McKinsey's State of AI 2025 (n=1,993): no more than 10% of respondents scale agents in any business function, and 7% in marketing and sales. Salesforce: 13% use agentic AI. HubSpot: 19.2% use agents end to end.
- **AI as volume for the same message.** Salesforce: 84% run generic campaigns despite 75% AI adoption.

## Stack
Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini, Clay, Meta Advantage+ and Advantage+ Creative, Klaviyo Marketing Agent, Midjourney/Firefly, n8n/Zapier, Profound/Peec.

## Open questions and contradictions
- **AI vs human creative.** Taboola finds parity under tight controls; NYU and INSEAD find AI ahead. Verdict: AI is at least at parity in paid creative as long as it doesn't read as AI.
- **Klarna figures are 2024 and self-reported;** "pivot back to humans" reports were unverified.
- **Lavender's "AI-assisted 5.1% vs fully AI 2.4%" statistic** could not be traced to a primary source; excluded.
