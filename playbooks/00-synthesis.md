# Synthesis: Marketing Engine Research, Phase 1

> Phase 1 research, compiled 5 Nov 2026. Evidence window 2025-2026. Every claim cites its source; full links in sources.md.

**Bottom line:** Across almost every channel, platforms and buyers now reward **distinct, human-feeling signal at volume**: diverse creative, named people, timely triggers and third-party citations. They penalise repetitive, interchangeable output: cold lists, near-duplicate ads, brand-page posts and unedited AI copy. The engine should automate *variety and timing*, not *volume of the same thing*.

## TL;DR
- **The decision is made before anyone talks to you.** B2B buyers pick the vendor they favoured before first contact 82% of the time (6sense 2026, n=4,611). Ahrefs' Feb 2026 update (300K keywords, Dec 2025 data) found an AI Overview correlates with a 58% lower CTR for the top-ranking page. Demand creation and AI-answer visibility now matter more than bottom-funnel capture.
- **Highest-leverage plays with hard numbers:** creative diversification on Meta (Meta calls it "the best lever" since April 2025); person-led distribution (LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads, founder content); signal-triggered outbound on authenticated domains; automated lifecycle flows (Klaviyo abandoned-cart revenue per recipient $3.07 vs about $0.05–0.11 for campaigns); Click-to-WhatsApp funnels in India; self-reported attribution paired with MMM and lift tests.
- **AI is table stakes, but real agent use is rare.** 86.4% of teams use AI (HubSpot 2026), yet only 13% use agentic AI (Salesforce). McKinsey's State of AI 2025 (n=1,993) finds no more than 10% of respondents scaling agents in any single function, and only 7% in marketing and sales. AI creative matches or beats human creative when it reads as human. In the NYU/Emory field study, disclosing AI involvement cut CTR by about 31.5% versus unlabelled human ads. Hunter's 2026 survey found 69% of decision-makers are bothered by AI-written email unless the output feels genuinely human.

## Cross-domain patterns
1. **Diversity beats volume.** Meta counts look-alike ads as one; top cold emailers micro-segment; AI citations spread across Reddit, LinkedIn, Wikipedia, G2. Generate distinct concepts, segments and surfaces.
2. **People beat logos.** TLAs are 22–47% cheaper per landing-page click than company-page ads (Kiin); personal profiles get roughly 2–4x company-page engagement (Sprout); 59% of LinkedIn AI citations are member content.
3. **Perceived humanity is a performance variable.** AI wins when it reads as human. It loses when labelled: disclosure cost about 31.5% of CTR in the NYU/Emory field study. It also loses when noticed: Hunter 2026 found 69% of decision-makers bothered unless the output feels genuinely human. Automate production; keep human voice and judgement at the surface.
4. **Timing beats targeting.** Signal outbound, cart flows and WhatsApp speed-to-lead monetise intent moments; cart RPR is 30–60x campaign RPR.
5. **Most demand is invisible to software** (Refine Labs 79% vs 3%; Omniscient 6.8x; Graphite 1% vs 9%). Without self-report plus MMM and lift tests, the engine defunds demand creation.
6. **Decisions form before contact or click** (6sense 82%; Ahrefs –58%).
7. **Owned channels are the cheapest repeatable conversions,** immune to reach collapse (LinkedIn reach down 50–60%).

## Default playbooks

### (a) Consumer product (e.g., sneaker marketplace, D2C)
1. **Weeks 0–2:** Meta CAPI; required HDYHAU at checkout; Klaviyo welcome, cart, browse and back-in-stock flows; WhatsApp API for drop alerts and cart nudges (₹0.115 utility / ₹0.86 marketing).
2. **Acquisition:** Advantage+ broad with 15–20+ live creatives across distinct motivations (price, scarcity/drop, culture/identity, social proof); human-reviewed AI variants; founder and creator UGC; CTWA for high-consideration items.
3. **Organic:** carousels for guides and product stories; Reels/TikTok for drops and culture; rotating creator bench.
4. **Measurement:** new-customer CAC vs platform ROAS; quarterly geo holdout; Meridian once spend passes about $1M a year.

### (b) B2B service (e.g., AI-native marketing agency)
1. **Outbound:** compliant secondary domains; Clay signals (funding, marketing hires, new CMO, ad-spend changes); human-edited 4–7 step sequences; target 3–5% replies, 1–2%+ positive.
2. **Founder-led LinkedIn:** 2–4 posts a week, documents, a LinkedIn newsletter; TLAs on best posts at 25–40% of LinkedIn spend as a start (Fractional Demand; validate on landing-page CTR).
3. **GEO:** numeric case studies on-site; seed Reddit, G2/Clutch, LinkedIn; track cited vs uncited.
4. **Conversion:** CTWA or instant booking for India SMB leads; speed-to-lead under 10 minutes.
5. **Measurement:** HDYHAU on booking form, reviewed monthly.

### (c) Creator/media brand (e.g., culture, music, events platform)
1. **Owned core:** beehiiv newsletter with welcome automations and Recommendations; WhatsApp community and broadcasts for event drops.
2. **Reach:** TikTok and Reels for discovery; carousels for event guides; Shorts as redistribution.
3. **Monetise intent:** event pages (entertainment median 12.3%; contests 47.5% for list building); email traffic converts best (28.6% ecommerce proxy).
4. **IRL loop:** events feed the list; the list sells the next event.
5. **Measurement:** HDYHAU at ticket checkout; RPR by flow.

## Final note
- **Most surprising:** only 26% of B2B buyers use LLMs to *discover* vendors despite near-universal use; TLA landing-page CTR equals company-page ads (the "6.9x" is engagement clicks); AI ads beat human ads only when they don't look like AI.
- **What I'd bet on:** Meta creative-diversity engines, person-led LinkedIn, human-edited signal outbound, WhatsApp-native funnels in India, self-reported attribution, and AIO *citation* as the new SEO KPI.
- **What I'd skip:** informational blog farms, fully autonomous AI SDRs, LinkedIn hashtags and polls, company-page-only social, Meta Robyn, and any framework (Hormozi, Dunford, JTBD) adopted on faith rather than tested.

## Known gaps in the evidence
- No 2025–26 primary TikTok ads CAC or creative benchmark.
- No controlled evidence for positioning/offer frameworks or pricing psychology.
- No India-specific CAC benchmarks beyond Meta's WhatsApp rate card; most India CTWA cases are vendor-reported.
- Category creation and creator ROI: anecdotal only.
