# Positioning and Messaging

> Phase 1 research, compiled 5 Nov 2026. Evidence window 2025-2026. Every claim cites its source; full links in sources.md.

**This domain has the weakest primary evidence.** No controlled 2025–26 study validating April Dunford's method, Hormozi's value equation or Jobs To Be Done (JTBD) was found. These are practitioner tools, not validated methods. Indirect evidence shows why positioning matters more than ever.

## Dead or overrated
- **Copy frameworks treated as proof.** None has published controlled lift data.
- **Visible "AI-generated" messaging** (–31.5% CTR on disclosure; 31% trust less).

## Stack
Gong/Fathom transcripts, HDYHAU fields, G2/Reddit review mining, Wynter-style message testing.

## Open questions and contradictions
- Frameworks are untested against each other. Use them as hypothesis generators and validate copy through cheap Meta creative tests and landing-page A/B tests.
