## Dead or overrated
- **A universal "good conversion rate."** Variance by source and offer outweighs industry.
- **Long mobile lead forms in India.** CTWA evidence, though vendor-heavy, points consistently the other way.

## Stack
Unbounce/Webflow/Framer, VWO/Optimizely, WhatsApp Business API with Flows, HubSpot forms, Hotjar/Clarity.

## Open questions and contradictions
- **Unbounce data age** (Jul 2023–Jul 2024) is older than ideal but still the largest dataset. LeadSystemsGo's claim that SaaS and ecommerce lead at 9–10% contradicts Unbounce's own pages (3.8%, 4.2%). Verdict: trust Unbounce.
- **CTWA "12–40%" conversion claims are vendor figures.** Meta-cited cases (Learnwise, House Hunt) are more credible but report cost per qualified lead.
