## Dead or overrated
- **Batch campaigns as the revenue engine.** Flows return about 18–100x campaign RPR (EightX from Klaviyo data).
- **Open rate as success metric.** Use placed-order rate and RPR.
- **Visible "AI-made" marketing.** Klaviyo/Datalily (8,000 consumers, Dec 2025): 7% trust a brand more, 31% less.

## Stack
Klaviyo, Postscript/Attentive, AiSensy/Interakt/Gupshup, beehiiv, SparkLoop.

## Open questions and contradictions
- **Klaviyo RPR varies by edition** (cart $3.07 vs $3.65; campaign $0.048 vs $0.11). Verdict: use the 2025 report PDF figures as the floor.
- **WhatsApp "200–300% ROI" and "150x" claims are vendor figures** (AiSensy); don't plan on them.
