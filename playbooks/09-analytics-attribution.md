## Dead or overrated
- **Multi-touch attribution as truth.** It cannot see podcasts, communities, DMs or AI answers.
- **Meta Robyn as a long-term bet.** AdExchanger sources say Meta is winding it down while Google ties sales KPIs to Meridian.

## Stack
Google Meridian (or Recast, PyMC-Marketing), GeoLift-style holdouts, GA4 AI-referral segment, HDYHAU, Meta CAPI, Triple Whale/Northbeam.

## Open questions and contradictions
- **Meridian neutrality.** AdExchanger frames it as a possible "Trojan horse." Verdict: use it but calibrate with your own lift tests and cross-check non-Google channels.
- **Refine Labs figures differ across write-ups** (79% vs 3%, or 78% vs 12%). The 60+ point gap is robust; quote Refine Labs' page.
- **Self-report recall bias** (Recast, Blend). Pair with software data.
