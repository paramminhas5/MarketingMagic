# CCD platform facts (from the catscandance.com codebase, Nov 2026) and the Indian scene-media landscape

## From the codebase (README, page copy, FAQs)
- Positioning in README: "India's definitive underground electronic music platform. Events · Artists · Scenes · Culture · Shop · Ticketing." Vision: "The Resident Advisor of India, built by the people who live it." Three audiences: fans, artists, promoters.
- Homepage meta: "Discover India's underground electronic music scene. Events in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi & Goa. Artist directory, genre guides, global scene origins and limited apparel drops."
- FAQ: Episodes are "RSVP-only underground dance music episodes" at venues including Bar Wild, Indiranagar. Genres: House, Disco, Jungle, Garage, Drum & Bass (artist page also lists breaks, dub techno; "We don't book commercial EDM or top-40 sets"). "Most episodes are free entry with name on the door." "Capacity is limited — RSVP early."
- Streetwear: "limited-edition streetwear drops rooted in underground dance music culture, screen-printed in Bangalore. No restocks." Pet streetwear: cat bandanas, pet bucket hats, cat treats; "Each pet streetwear drop is limited."
- For artists: "A platform, not just a set time — curated crowd, premium content, real audience growth"; artists get full set recording, photo/video assets, "a curated room of 200-400 people who listen", a feature on playlists and the newsletter. Booking: send a 30-60 minute mix, Instagram and one line.
- For venues: "Episodes are RSVP-only, capped, curated lineups. The crowd is here for the music, which means longer dwell time, better F&B spend, and content the venue can use for months after."
- For investors: "a Bangalore-based culture company — events, drops, playlists, community. A multi-revenue ecosystem"; "Community-led distribution lowers CAC"; "Scales city-by-city, category-by-category".
- CCD x Social sponsor page: season finale "MEGA — GRAND FORMAT SHOW. Full outdoor stage. 2,000+ people. Pet runway. Agility finals." (date TBA)
- Cats Can Care: searchable directory of India's top 100 animal welfare NGOs.
- Platform features live: Discover (nightly scraper of Skillbox, District, Insider, HighApe with Claude Haiku scoring; promoter submissions; editorial picks), recommendations (For You, Trending, Editor's Picks, This Weekend), weekly digest email (Mondays), artist directory with 7-tab profiles (Home, Gigs, Connections, Journey, Stats, EPK, Book), 6-month availability calendar and booking, promoter portal, fan XP/points, RSVP with WhatsApp opt-in, early-access email capture, 14 SEO blog posts, scene city pages, genre pages, global scene pages, Bengaluru scene guide, Shopify shop, Instagram feed, videos, playlists.
- Roadmap gaps noted in the README: points redemption (users see "redemption launching soon", called "a broken promise"), promoter analytics, 24h pre-event WhatsApp reminder, attended/check-in flow, per-event SEO pages for curated events (currently link offsite), first-party ticketing v2.

## Scene media / platform landscape (web search, 2026-11-05)
- Wild City (thewildcity.com): "India's essential online music guide", with an artist and business directory (e.g. Skillbox, Boxout Weekender listings).
- Resident Advisor (ra.co): has India city guides (e.g. ra.co/guide/in/delhi).
- Skillbox: ticketing/discovery for independent music; Warner Music India took a minority stake (Digital Music News, Nov 2024).
- Homegrown (homegrown.co.in): Indian youth culture media covering electronic events (e.g. Milkman anniversary).
- District, Insider, HighApe, SortMyScene: ticketing apps running Meta ads for promoters' nights.
- Beatchain x Radio City India: "MuzArtDisco" independent artist discovery platform (Music Business World Wide).
- weraveyou.com (May 2025): "India's underground electronic scene is growing: how far can it go"
