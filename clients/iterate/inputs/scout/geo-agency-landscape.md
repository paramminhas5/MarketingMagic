## Source: Pepper, "Best GEO Agencies in India" (pepper.inc/blog/best-geo-agencies-india) — self-published ranking, Pepper ranks itself #1
- Pepper (Mumbai, 2017): platform + growth team + content program for enterprises, 250+ clients; case studies Apollo 24/7 (200% organic traffic, 2.5x new users, 77% organic revenue growth in a year), CRED, Piramal (45% monthly organic growth). Pricing custom/enterprise.
- TripleDart (2021): B2B SaaS specialist with dedicated GEO and AEO lines. No proof given.
- Schbang (Mumbai, 2015, 1,000+ staff): creative/media/tech agency; GEO practice "still developing"; 300+ brands (Fevicol, Jio, Garnier); no AI-citation results.
- AdLift (Gurgaon, Bangalore, Mumbai, SF): SEO agency extending into GEO; PayPal, Shopify, Snapdeal, Oyo, Fortis; no GEO outcomes.
- WATConsult (Dentsu): full-service digital with a GEO add-on; no public GEO case studies.
- PageTraffic (Delhi, 20+ yrs SEO, 120+ people): extending into GEO; no GEO outcomes.
- Buyer guidance in the article: "proof over pitch", sector fit over reputation, execution over measurement. Five questions: can the agency show real AI citations (not just traffic) and what earned them; does it know your sector's trust requirements; which engines does it track (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews); does it execute or only measure; can it serve your geographies. Suggested free first step: run your buyer questions through ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini yourself and see if you appear.
