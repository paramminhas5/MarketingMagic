# Strategy: Iterate

_2026-11-05 · Strategist_

## Positioning
> For culture-led consumer brands in India, Iterate is the AI visibility and growth pod that gets you cited in AI answers and then does the work across search, social, paid and community, run by operators rather than account managers.

**Alternatives:**
- Keep the existing SEO agency and add their new GEO add-on
- Buy a GEO tracking tool (e.g. Writesonic) and run it in-house
- Hire a large full-service agency (Schbang-style) with a developing GEO practice
- Do nothing and hope AI answers pick the brand up

**Unique:**
- Operator-led: founded by Param Minhas, 15+ years across AI, marketing, venture and consumer startups, co-founder of SoleSearch
- Small AI-augmented pods instead of headcount
- Five layers in one team: discovery (AI answers and search), capture (clipping, UGC, social, priced on outcomes), organic, paid, community (recurring offline events)
- Own frameworks: AI Visibility Architecture (AVA) and GEO/AEO, plus structured data and llms.txt builds

**Value:**
- Brands get found and trusted in AI answers, with citations shown, not traffic charts
- Findings get executed by the same team, across search, social, paid and community
- Senior operator attention without enterprise-agency overhead

**Best fit:** Founders and marketing heads of culture-led Indian consumer brands (D2C, lifestyle, travel, mobility, apparel) that already spend on marketing, care how they show up in AI answers, and want an operator-led team that ships.

**Category:** AI visibility and growth pods

## Offers
- **AI Answer Check (free)**: Prospect submits brand and 3 competitors; Iterate runs 10 buyer questions through ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini and sends a one-page result with screenshots within 48 hours. Hypothesis: A free, concrete check of how the brand shows up for 10 buyer questions books discovery calls better than a generic audit offer. Risk: Manual effort per lead; mitigate by templating with the engine
- **90-day AI visibility pilot**: Audit plus fixes plus citation seeding for 90 days at a fixed monthly fee, with a dated citation report at days 0, 45 and 90. Hypothesis: A fixed 3-month pilot matches how buyers are being told to buy GEO and lowers the commitment barrier. Risk: Citation gains may take longer than 90 days for some categories
- **Growth pod**: Monthly pod across the five layers; capture priced on outcomes. Hypothesis: Pilot clients convert to a pod once they see citations move. Risk: Scope creep across five layers for a small team

## Tests
- The free AI Answer Check gets more calls than a generic 'book a call' CTA · metric: Calls booked per 100 landing-page visitors · min sample: 300 visitors per arm · kill: If the check converts below the call CTA after 300 visitors per arm, drop it
- 'Proof over pitch' framing beats 'invisible in AI' framing in TLAs · metric: Landing-page click cost · min sample: 30 landing-page clicks per arm · kill: Pause the losing motivation after 2x the other's cost at 30 clicks
- Outbound triggered by a new marketing leader or recent funding replies better than static lists · metric: Positive reply rate · min sample: 200 contacts per segment · kill: Stop a segment under 1% positive replies at 200 contacts

## Risks
- No public, nameable case studies (NDA): proof has to come from dated citation screenshots and generic descriptions
- Category is crowded with self-published rankings; Iterate needs to appear on those citation surfaces
- Iterate's own AI and search visibility is unmeasured (Ahrefs plan insufficient)
- Founder time is the bottleneck for LinkedIn and sales calls
