# Market brief: Iterate

_2026-11-05 · Scout_

## Summary
India's GEO / AI-visibility agency market is crowded with SEO and full-service agencies bolting GEO onto old offers, and almost none of them publish AI-citation results. Rankings are self-published (Pepper and Texta each rank themselves first). Buyers are being told to demand proof of real AI citations, sector fit and execution, and to run a 3-month pilot. That gap, plus Iterate's operator-led pods and its five-layer model (discovery through community), is the opening: be the agency that shows citations, not traffic, and that executes beyond search.

**Category:** AI visibility (GEO/AEO) and growth execution for consumer brands in India

## Competitors

**Angle:** Enterprise content platform plus growth team; publishes GEO agency rankings with itself first

Strengths:
- 250+ clients
- Named organic-growth case studies (Apollo 24/7, CRED, Piramal)
- Owns the 'best GEO agencies India' content

Weaknesses:
- Proof is organic traffic, not AI citations
- Enterprise-only pricing

Channels: SEO content, self-published rankings

**Angle:** Bangalore pure-play GEO platform plus services; publishes 'top 10 GEO agencies' with itself first

Strengths:

Weaknesses:
- Enterprise tier starts at ₹3,00,000+/month
- Ranking draws on its own data

Channels: SEO content, self-published rankings

## Audience
### Marketing heads at Indian consumer and D2C brands with existing SEO/social budgets
Pains:
- Unsure whether and how the brand shows up in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Overviews
- Agencies sell GEO without showing citation results

Desires:
- Proof of real AI citations
- Execution, not just audits
- Integration with existing SEO, social and paid

Objections:
- Retainers with 3-6 month commitments
- Self-published rankings make vendors hard to compare

Where they are: Google search for 'GEO agency India', LinkedIn, Indian marketing trade press

### Founders of growth-stage consumer brands without a large marketing team
Pains:
- Can't afford enterprise agency tiers
- Need demand, not just reports

Desires:
- Operator-led team that ships
- Outcome-linked pricing

Objections:
- Under ₹50,000/month budgets get pointed to freelancers

Where they are: LinkedIn, founder networks, events

## Opportunities
- **Publish a citation-first case study format: before/after AI answers for real buyer questions, with screenshots and dates** (high): Both landscape sources note competitors show traffic, not AI citations; buyers are told to ask for citations
- **Free 'AI answer check' as the lead magnet: run 10 buyer questions through ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini and show where the brand appears** (high): Pepper's own advice tells buyers to do exactly this before choosing an agency
- **A 3-month pilot offer priced in the mid-market band (₹1-3 lakh/month) with execution across discovery, capture and community** (medium): Texta's tiers and its recommendation of a 3-month pilot
- **Get Iterate onto the citation surfaces that AI answers likely pull from for 'GEO agency India': directories, trade press and LinkedIn** (medium): Search results for the category are dominated by self-published lists and trade press
